Features

Everything that happens on the pitch — in one app.

SINAI Club was built on the pitch, together with coaches, around what really happens at training — not around spreadsheets in an office.

Professional session planning: for a session, a month, a season

Want to focus on finishing for the next month? Pick a topic and a duration, and get a plan in stages: teach, practise, apply it in the game, and a test at the end. Drills rotate, and every session also keeps another topic in the mix so the rest of the skills don't slip. You can also build a season plan with a topic for every week.

Focused monthly planSeason planAny session lengthA diagram for every drillShared folder

Tap-to-rate, all session long

In every drill and the closing game: a quick tag, a score for an attribute, or a quick round that rates the whole team on exactly what the drill trains. Timed tests go onto the card too.

A player card, just like Football Manager

Every rating adds up to a 1–20 profile across technical, mental and physical attributes, with a monthly trend, position, preferred foot and weak foot. See exactly who is improving, and at what.

  • Attributes are age-appropriate: kindergarten looks different from youth
  • The club decides whether parents and kids see the numbers
  • Values based on only a few observations are flagged, so no decisions are made on a guess

Automatic split into balanced teams or pick them yourself, the score, and ratings — all without leaving the pitch.

Attendance and parent communication

Take attendance with a tap, and send ready-made messages to parents and the team — written from the data and sent via WhatsApp in one click.

An alert before a kid drops out

A kid who misses two sessions in a row is flagged for the coach, along with who's back after an absence and who's injured. So you can step in early, before they quietly disappear from the team.

Motivation that comes from effort

Personal goals for every player and attributes that improve from session to session — rewarding hard work and progress, not just talent.
